QUAN TRONG:
index.html la giao dien WebApp. De su dung onlne, can khoi dong may chu FastAPI.
Mo file index.html bang nhap dup chi hien thi giao dien va canh bao, KHONG the
kiem tra/cham bai/luu diem/lay ngan hang cau hoi neu khong co may chu.

CHAY THU TAI MAY:
1. Giai nen ZIP thanh mot thu muc rieng.
2. Cai Python 3.11+, mo Terminal trong thu muc du an.
3. pip install -r requirements.txt
4. Thiet lap ADMIN_PASSWORD va SESSION_SECRET; xem .env.example.
5. uvicorn server:APP --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000
6. Mo http://127.0.0.1:8000/index.html
